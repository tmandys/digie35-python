Final check before capturing

Please unsure the following settings and conditions are correct:

  • Camera is in the expected shooting mode Usually Av (Aperture priority) with manual focus, minimal ISO (non-auto!)
  • Image stabilization is turned off, if available
  • Lens hood is attached to reduce side light or reflection
  • Adapter is fully inserted and securely attached
  • Lens is properly focused on the film grain, using manual focus, minimal aperture (e.g. f/4) and magnifier. Focused when adapter flattening press was down, if provided.
    Note: Focusing is done with the lens wide open - minimal f-number - for best accuracy.
  • The film passes symetrically under the lens and perforation holes are partially visible on both sides.
  • Focus somewhere between the center and edges to compensate for any film curvature
  • The working aperture is set to around f/8 to increase depth of field

These steps help ensure consistently sharp, high-quality images.

Backlight settings

A profile records how changes to individual LED channels affect the RGB values seen by your camera. Create it without film, using fixed camera exposure and white balance, and use a profile measured with the same light and camera settings. It provides the response data for automatic white-balance correction; selecting a profile does not yet adjust the LEDs.

Backlight calibration
  1. Remove the film and fill the entire preview with the illuminated diffuser. Keep holders, borders and other objects out of the frame.
  2. Use a camera profile with Calibration settings to fix exposure and white balance (recommended 5000 K) and disable AWB or other automatic image adjustments. If the profile cannot control a setting on your camera, set it manually: use manual exposure or reliable AE lock, and keep shutter time, aperture, ISO, and white balance fixed throughout calibration.
  3. Analyze is enabled automatically and restored afterwards. Calibration measures the central 90% of the source snapshot width and height, ignoring 5% at each edge for both averaging and clipping checks. The darkest and brightest 10% of values in each RGB channel are excluded from the average to reduce dust and reflection effects. Clipping is checked before this exclusion. The eyedropper is not used. Precise focus is not required on a uniform diffuser; disable autofocus.
  4. Enter a unique profile name. During measurement, keep the camera and light settings unchanged.
K
Enter the settings used on the camera, including any A/B or G/M shift. These values are saved with the profile; Digie35 does not yet read or set camera WB or exposure.
One profile covers the whole lightbox, independent of the selected LED mode. Every supported visible channel is measured alone with the others off, including both 3000 K and 5000 K whites on tunable-white lights. Initial LED gains start at 1. LED exposure is chosen once for the reference mixture; isolated channel gains are adjusted for usable measurements with PWM headroom. The mixture response is measured separately because preview RGB is not linear. Keep camera exposure and WB fixed for the entire run. Original light settings and Analyze state are restored afterwards.
